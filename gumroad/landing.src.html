Features

From “where was that loop?” to dragged into your DAW in seconds.

Fits your track

Type a tempo. Pick a key. Everything follows.

Set the project tempo in the title bar (nudge it with arrow keys or tap it) and pick a key from the key wheel. Every loop you audition plays in time and in tune.

  • Sync: Stretch keeps the pitch, Repitch speeds up like tape. Saga picks half or double time when that's closer.
  • Key matching shifts tonal samples by the smallest interval, with formant preservation for vocals.
  • Scale lock: [ ] step a sample through the project key's scale.

Shape it, then drag it

Press E for a real editor, not a preview.

A full-width, zoomable waveform with a bar ruler, loop regions snapped to bars or beats, reverse, fades, gain, normalize and a metronome locked to your tempo.

  • Drag anything (a row, the preview or the editor output) into your DAW or Finder.
  • Renders match the preview because both share the same code. They're kept in Music/Saga/Renders, so your projects keep working.
  • Save variation adds it to your library with the new tempo and key in its name.
Find similar & the sound map

Press G to find sounds like this one. Press M to see them all.

Get the closest-sounding samples in your library, compared overall or by timbre, pitch or envelope. Drop in any audio file, or hum, beatbox or play into the mic to search by sound.

  • The sound map lays out every sample so similar sounds sit together, colored by category, brightness or loudness.
  • Lasso a group straight into a collection. Your search and filters light up their matches.
The Lab

Press H for a harmony workspace that knows your library.

37 scales on a pitch circle and keyboard, with their chords and color notes. Make one the project key, or list the samples that fit it, best fit first.

  • Progressions: sketch 2, 4 or 8 bars and drag the MIDI clip into your DAW.
  • Key finder for any sample, or notes you pick on a keyboard.
  • Tune a one-shot: measure a kick or 808 and tune it to the key.
Filters

Narrow ten thousand loops to the ten that work.

Filter by one-shot or loop, category, tempo (with half and double time), key with Camelot compatibility, length, channels, format, sample rate and tags. Histograms show how your library is spread before you pick a range.

  • Watched folders: new packs appear on their own.
  • Favorites, collections and tags wait for unplugged drives to come back.

FAQ

Questions, answered.

Which computers does Saga run on?

macOS on both Apple Silicon and Intel Macs, and Windows.

Will Saga reorganize or touch my sample folders?

No. Saga indexes samples where they are and never changes, copies or renames your files. Processed versions you drag out are rendered to a separate folder, Music/Saga/Renders.

Does it work with my DAW?

Saga runs alongside your DAW as its own app. You drag samples, rendered loops and MIDI clips into any DAW or folder that accepts dropped files. A mini player window can stay on top next to your DAW.

How accurate is tempo and key detection?

Saga trusts file names first, then embedded loop data (ACID chunks in WAV, Apple Loops in AIFF and CAF), then the audio itself. Values detected from audio are marked with ≈ so you know to check them. On real packs, detection gets the tempo right for 70–80% of loops, and keys are only shown when the fit is strong.

Will it slow down my sessions?

Analysis runs at low priority on half your CPU cores, and Saga only holds the audio device while you're previewing, so your DAW keeps control.

Does Saga send my library anywhere?

No. Everything happens on your computer. The only thing Saga fetches from the internet is the update check, and you can turn that off in Settings.

How long does the first scan take?

Search works as soon as your folders are added. Saga then listens to every sample once in the background, which takes a few minutes for a big library.

Is Saga really free?

Yes. Download it from GitHub and every feature is there. A donation of any amount through Gumroad or PayPal helps fund its development, but it doesn't unlock anything or send a download link.
